On The Call

Here's exactly
what happens.

No pitch theatre. No "high-pressure close." A real 30-minute working session about your physique, your bloodwork, and where the leverage actually is. If we're a fit, we'll talk structure at the end. If we're not, you'll still walk away with direction.

0–5 min
Where you actually are.
Quick read on your training, nutrition, sleep, enhancement status, and bloodwork (from what you sent me). I'll already have your stats — this is just confirmation, not intake.
5–15 min
Diagnostic — what's actually capping you.
Most guys at your level aren't held back by effort. We dig into what your bloodwork is saying, where your physiology is leaking output, and which variable is the real bottleneck (it's rarely the one you think).
15–25 min
How I'd actually work with you.
Specific to your situation — training adjustments, nutrition framework, bloodwork roadmap, enhancement strategy if relevant, recovery and joint/CNS work. You see the playbook, not a pitch deck.
25–30 min
Structure, pricing, decision.
If coaching makes sense for both of us, I'll walk you through how we'd structure it and the investment. You decide on your own time — no surprise upsells, no "today only" pressure.
